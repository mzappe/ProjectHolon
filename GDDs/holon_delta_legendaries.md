# Pokémon Holon — Delta Legendary & Mythical Reference

A complete reference table for every legendary and mythical Pokémon in Generations 1–4,
assessing their potential as Delta forms in the Holon post-game. Organized by generation,
with TCG canon status, thematic fit, suggested Delta typing, and narrative hooks.

---

## How to Read This Table

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **TCG Canon** | Whether a Delta form of this Pokémon appeared in the actual EX Delta Species arc sets |
| **Holon Fit** | How naturally the Pokémon's identity connects to Holon's themes (radiation, space, transformation, EM, ancient mystery) |
| **Suggested Delta Type(s)** | Possible type mutations — not definitive, just directional brainstorming |
| **Narrative Hook** | Why this Pokémon arriving at / being drawn to Holon is interesting |

**Fit ratings:**
- ⭐⭐⭐ — Strong fit, use seriously
- ⭐⭐ — Solid fit with the right framing
- ⭐ — Workable but lower priority
- ➖ — Weak fit or actively conflicts with Holon's identity

## Generation 3

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Regirock** | No | ⭐⭐ | Steel / Electric | Ancient construct awakened by the radiation. Its rock nature replaced by metal — the Tower's metallic resonance rewriting its composition. |
| **Regice** | No | ⭐⭐ | Fire / Fighting | Ice replaced by fire. The cold precision of Regice as a Delta becomes aggressive heat instead. |
| **Registeel** | No | ⭐ | Rock / Psychic | Already Steel type — Delta transformation would need to move it away from Metal to be interesting. Less natural fit given the TCG's established Metal secondary type pattern. |
| **Latias** | ✅ Yes (dual Metal δ, EX Delta Species) | ⭐⭐⭐ | Metal secondary (established) / alternate: Fire/Metal | TCG canon. The eon duo arriving together is a strong paired encounter. Latias as the more approachable of the two — found first. |
| **Latios** | ✅ Yes (dual Metal δ, EX Delta Species) | ⭐⭐⭐ | Metal secondary (established) / alternate: Electric/Metal | TCG canon. Paired with Latias. More aggressive and harder to reach. The player encounters Latias first, which teaches them how to approach Latios. |
| **Kyogre** | No | ⭐⭐⭐ | Electric / Dark | The sea incarnate, drawn by oceanic EM propagation from the Tower's signal. Its water nature replaced by electric or dark. Kyogre as a Delta is one of the most visually dramatic possibilities in the game — the primal sea rendered as something alien. |
| **Groudon** | No | ⭐⭐⭐ | Water / Ghost | Counterpart to Kyogre. The continent-raiser as a Delta that inverts its drought-causing nature. Groudon and Kyogre as Delta paired legendaries are the highest-stakes encounter pair the post-game could offer. |
| **Rayquaza** | ✅ Yes (Fire/Metal δ, EX Delta Species) | ⭐⭐⭐ | Fire/Metal (established) | TCG canon and already woven into the main story as territorial threat. A Delta Rayquaza is the most emotionally resonant escalation — the guardian transformed by the phenomenon it was trying to stop. Does it still behave territorially? Does it recognize what it is now? |
| **Jirachi** | No | ⭐⭐⭐ | Dark / Electric | Dormancy/awakening cycle directly echoes Deoxys's arc. Woken by radiation it mistook for its comet trigger. Delta transformation before it fully orients — it wakes wrong. Strong candidate for the true final boss of the main story (false ending reveal). |
| **Deoxys** | ✅ Yes (all four formes δ, EX Holon Phantoms) | ✅ Core game | N/A — already the main story | All four Delta formes are the main game's central encounters. Not a post-game candidate. |

## Summary: Priority Tiers for the Post-Game

### Tier 1 — TCG Canon (Use These, They're Lore-Legitimate)

| Pokémon | TCG Set | Established Delta Type |
|---|---|---|
| Mewtwo δ | EX Delta Species, EX Holon Phantoms | Lightning |
| Rayquaza δ | EX Delta Species | Fire / Metal |
| Latias δ | EX Delta Species | (dual Metal secondary) |
| Latios δ | EX Delta Species | (dual Metal secondary) |
| Mew ☆ δ | EX Dragon Frontiers | Water |

### Tier 2 — Strongest New Delta Candidates

| Pokémon | Why |
|---|---|
| Jirachi δ | Dormancy echo, false ending reveal candidate, most thematically connected to main story structure |
| Rayquaza δ | Already in Tier 1 — also the most emotionally resonant post-game escalation given its main story role |
| Suicune δ | Holon Lake connection, purification inverted, anomalous Delta patterns there already suggest its presence |
| Lugia δ | Containment disrupted — one of the most dramatically interesting Delta concepts |
| Giratina δ | Best creation trio fit for Holon's threatening tone; presence causes passive wrongness |
| Darkrai δ | Light masquerading as dark, or dark masquerading as light — strong inversion concept |
| Cresselia δ | Night sky / aurora connection, direct visual fit, light-into-nightmare inversion |
| Kyogre δ | Oceanic EM propagation, primal scale, visually dramatic Delta transformation |
| Celebi δ | Closes the loop on Mirage Forest; the forest that seemed untouched has been changed |

### Tier 3 — Worth Considering Given the Right Structure

Raikou δ, Entei δ, Ho-Oh δ, Groudon δ, Heatran δ, Shaymin δ, lake trio (Uxie / Mesprit / Azelf δ), Articuno δ, Zapdos δ, Moltres δ

### Skip

Arceus, Phione, Regigigas (low priority), Registeel (type conflict with Delta Metal pattern)
