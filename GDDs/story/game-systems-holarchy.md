# Project Holon — Systems Holarchy

A holarchy: each level is a **whole** made of the level below, and simultaneously a **part** of the level above. Nothing on this list stands alone — every system both contains and is contained.

```
COSMIC          the universe-scale mystery: Mew, deep space, origin of life
  └─ DEOXYS         an alien intrusion into that mystery — one cosmic actor made local
       └─ ENVIRONMENTAL   the region's ecology, warped by Deoxys/Delta-Species radiation
            └─ TECH           the machinery (Research Tower, EM tracking) built to study/exploit it
                 └─ BUSINESS      the corporate/institutional funding & control behind that tech
                      └─ HUMAN         the settlements, staff, and relationships the institutions organize
                           └─ POKEMON      the partner creatures bonded within that human world
                                └─ PERSONAL    the player's own identity, choices, and arc
```

## Reading it both directions

**Top-down (whole → parts):** Cosmic contains Deoxys as one thread; Deoxys's fallout shapes the Environmental layer; Environmental anomalies are what Tech was built to track; Tech is funded and directed by Business; Business staffs and governs the Human settlements; Human communities are where Pokémon partnerships form; and all of it converges, moment to moment, on one Personal experience — the player's.

**Bottom-up (part → whole):** the Personal story is inseparable from a Pokémon bond; that bond only exists inside a Human community; that community is shaped by Business interests; Business exists to run Tech; Tech was built in response to Environmental anomalies; those anomalies trace back to Deoxys; and Deoxys is itself just one local instance of the larger Cosmic mystery the whole game is chasing.

## Per-layer summary

| Layer | What it is | Contains (parts) | Contained by (whole) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cosmic** | The universe-level mystery — Mew, deep space, the origin question the whole game is built around | Deoxys | — (outermost) |
| **Deoxys** | The alien/meteor thread — a cosmic actor that has landed and gone local | Environmental | Cosmic |
| **Environmental** | Region ecology & geography, and how it's being altered (Delta Species spread, Power Spots) | Tech | Deoxys |
| **Tech** | The Research Tower, EM tracking, mutation science — built to study/exploit the environmental anomaly | Business | Environmental |
| **Business** | Corporate/institutional funding and control (Devon Corp-style overlap, lab hierarchy) | Human | Tech |
| **Human** | Settlements, staff ranks, NPC relationships, social fabric | Pokemon | Business |
| **Pokemon** | Individual creatures and the partnerships formed with them | Personal | Human |
| **Personal** | The player character — identity, choices, internal arc | — (innermost) | Pokemon |

## Why this ordering

Each layer is scoped to sit **inside** the one above it and **around** the one below it, so nothing is a dead end or a floating category:

- *Personal* is the innermost holon — it only means anything through a *Pokemon* bond.
- *Pokemon* partnerships only exist because a *Human* community made them possible (labs, towns, trainers).
- *Human* social structure is organized and funded by *Business*.
- *Business* exists to build and run *Tech*.
- *Tech* was built as a direct response to an *Environmental* anomaly.
- *Environmental* mutation is fallout from *Deoxys*.
- *Deoxys* is one visible piece of the larger *Cosmic* mystery.

Any plot beat, character, or system in the game should be placeable on this chain — and should visibly touch its neighbors above and below.
