# Holon Region Pixel Map Mockup v1 — Generation Prompt

Built-in image generation mode. Use case: `stylized-concept`.

Create a highly accurate, readable top-down pixel-art world map of the complete Holon region for route spacing and game planning. It should look like a polished in-game Town Map / region-map screen, using crisp hard-edged pixel clusters, a limited indexed-color feeling, and 16-bit handheld-era terrain tiles. Avoid painterly, anti-aliased, photorealistic, perspective, or parchment-map styling.

Use a wide landscape canvas with ocean filling the background and generous separation between landmasses. Put the main continental landmass at left/center and let the ocean route progress toward island zones on the right. Make paths, waterways, cliffs, bridges, ports, and chokepoints clearly legible. Show routes as thin connected light paths and keep the overview planning-first.

The mainland is a vast, misty Jurassic Mirage Forest surrounding the central power spot. Include a coastal arrival edge and small unnamed starting port, Outer Mirage Forest, Sand Basin, an overgrown Ruins of Progress railway belt, Cursed Shrine cave mouth and Strange Cave, Deep Mirage Forest, the Power Spot clearing with Full Flame, Power Tree and giant stump, Fossil Grove, and three small hidden Regi chamber markers.

Place Holon Village at the absolute central power spot. Its plan is circular: an outer ring of trees, a canal through the diameter feeding two circular waterways, nine small red-roof buildings with farms or orchards, and the Research Tower at exact center. The four-story triangular tower has an upward-facing dish and three small domed satellites in triangular formation, forming a Metal Energy-like three-node symbol from above. Put moss-covered Holon Ruins elsewhere in or at the edge of the village.

Place Holon Lake directly south of Holon Village. It is a large misty lake bordered by deciduous woods, long grass, marsh, and an intersecting jagged blue mountain range. Include partially submerged Stone Dome Grove, three stone pillars around a glowing blue central sphere, antenna towers fixed to high cliffs, subtle pipes and machinery beneath the water, and narrow columns of EM light.

Give Mirage Forest distinct peripheral gated branches: a glacial coast with floating ice sheets and Ice Plateau, a volcanic edge with smoking calderas and lava, and a reef edge descending through Coral Coast into the Abyssal Trench. Include a clear Act 4 mainland port/passage after Holon Lake as the island embarkation point.

Across the ocean, show Crystal Isles as a remote tropical volcanic archipelago with rocky palm-lined beaches, jungle mixed with thin conifers, smoking volcanoes, a cyclone motif, red/orange crystals in volcanic fissures, green/purple crystals in caves, and pale-blue crystals along beaches and coral. Mark the Holon Circle as a circular crater with three tall pink-purple crystals in triangular formation. Put the Volcano Observatory on the largest volcano's rim.

Farther beyond Crystal Isles, show Dragon Frontiers as a lower rocky island plus a separate Floating Island. The lower island has reddish-orange sandstone canyons and pillars, sparse scrub, a small pine forest, a swamp by inland water, an ancient diamond-carving site, the Holon Legacy (three tall stones around one short center stone), and a huge right-angled Sunken Stone half-submerged in the sea. The Floating Island is ringed by white clouds, has jagged spires, and transitions into dark starry near-space.

Add Delta Horizon as a small isolated postgame island farther out, with beach, jungle, highland, a small trainer settlement and port, and four distinct facilities: Delta Colosseum, Delta Preserve, crystal-cave Delta Labyrinth, and linear coastal/jungle Delta Passage.

Use discreet glowing crystal-node lines to connect ancient sites across Mirage Forest, Holon Village, Holon Lake, Crystal Isles, and Dragon Frontiers, suggesting one buried regional network without obscuring travel routes.

Render only these major labels in a clean small blocky pixel font, spelled exactly: `MIRAGE FOREST`, `HOLON VILLAGE`, `HOLON LAKE`, `CRYSTAL ISLES`, `DRAGON FRONTIERS`, `FLOATING ISLAND`, `DELTA HORIZON`. Use numbered markers for micro-landmarks.

Palette: deep jungle greens, misty teal, cool blue mountains and glacial water, warm ochre sand, rust-orange railway, volcanic crimson, crystal cyan and magenta, red sandstone, pale cloud white, and dark indigo space. Use subtle aurora/EM accents while preserving daytime readability.

Constraints: all five canonical zones plus Delta Horizon visible; Holon Lake directly south of Holon Village; Holon Village and Tower centered within Mirage Forest; Crystal Isles reached by ocean after the mainland coastal port; Dragon Frontiers farther beyond; no characters, Pokémon, logos, watermark, extra regions, random city sprawl, modern satellite realism, or UI screenshot frame.
