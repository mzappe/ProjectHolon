# Pokémon Holon Legends

**What do we owe the things we discover?**

Deep in Holon, familiar Pokémon carry unfamiliar types. Beyond the research settlements lie ancient forests, restless coastlines, and signs of a region still being discovered. Pack your field gear, find your partner, and step into a place where even your first encounter can make you rethink your team.

Pokémon Holon Legends is a Pokémon Emerald ROM hack inspired by the Pokémon TCG’s Delta Species era: a new adventure built around exploration, field research, and the thrill of discovery.

## The adventure

- **Discover Delta Species.** Familiar Pokémon reimagined with different types and custom artwork, opening up new ways to build your team.
- **Explore Holon.** Five distinct zones inspired by the Delta Species card sets, from deep wilderness to remote islands, with their own landscapes and Pokémon to discover.
- **Follow the mystery.** An original story woven through research, exploration, and the people who call Holon home. The answers are yours to uncover.
- **Take the long way.** Side quests and discoveries that give you reasons to explore beyond the main story.
- **A Pokédex faithful to the TCG.** A roster matching the Pokémon featured across the five Holon-based TCG sets.
- **Challenge the Holon League.** Eight Gym Leaders and a regional Champion to put your team to the test.

## A familiar game with a fresh feel

- **Pokémon by your side.** Follower Pokémon accompany you in the overworld.
- **Reusable TMs.** Experiment with movesets without using up your TMs.
- **Choose your shiny rate.** Pick from five settings when starting a new save, from rare encounters to more frequent finds.
- **An expanded Pokédex interface.** An HGSS-style Pokédex with dark mode for browsing your discoveries.
- **Updated battle foundations.** A Generation VI mechanics baseline beneath the classic GBA presentation.
- **Holon’s own visual identity.** Custom trainer artwork, a new title screen, animated starfield menus, and a redesigned start menu with location, time, weather, and time since your last save.

## Development

Holon Legends is in active development and is not yet ready as a playable adventure. Features, artwork, and content may change as the game takes shape.

For source setup and build instructions, see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).
